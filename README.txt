TEA CLUB SAHIWAL — FIVE-PAGE WEBSITE

PAGES
- index.html: Home
- menu.html: Full interactive menu with category filters
- about.html: About Tea Club
- gallery.html: Café photo gallery
- contact.html: Location, Google Maps, WhatsApp and calling

PREVIEW
Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a browser. Keep all HTML files, site.css, site.js, menu.js and the assets folder together.

IMPORTANT
The menu is transcribed from the printed menu photo supplied by the user. A few entries/prices may need management confirmation. Contact numbers and WhatsApp destination must be verified before publishing. Confirm permission to use the café logo and photos.
